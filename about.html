About This Project

Making Everyday Browser Tracking Visible in Plain English

Every time you open a webpage on your phone or laptop, dozens of automated advertising systems inspect small technical clues about your screen, graphics chip, time zone, and browser settings—all in less than a fifth of a second. Most people never see this happen because it runs invisibly in the background.

Our Core Rule: Show, Don't Collect

BrowserClues.live was built so anyone can see what their own browser gives away without handing their personal data over to yet another website. Our live device check runs 100% locally inside your own browser window. We set zero tracking cookies, keep zero server logs of your device clues, and never ask for your name or email address.
