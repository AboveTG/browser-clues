Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026 • Applies to BrowserClues.live and Research.BrowserClues.live

The Short Version

Our browser privacy scanner runs 100% locally inside your own web browser. We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell your device scan results, receipt images, or custom demand letters.

1. How Our Local Browser Check Works

When you visit BrowserClues.live, standard JavaScript running inside your own browser reads basic display and hardware properties (such as screen resolution, time zone, graphics renderer name, and Do-Not-Track settings) so you can see them on your screen. That information never leaves your device and is never sent to any database owned by us. When you download a PNG receipt image, your browser draws that image locally on your own screen.

2. First-Party Cookies and Local Storage

BrowserClues.live does not set any first-party tracking cookies, advertising identifiers, or cross-site tracking pixels. When you click a link from our scanner to a matched guide on research.browserclues.live, we pass a general topic label after a # symbol in the web address (for example, #clue=open-tracking). Web browsers never send anything after a # symbol to web servers.

3. Advertising and Google AdSense

To keep our educational tools and guides free for everyone, we use Google AdSense to display advertisements on our pages. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to visitors based on their visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google My Ad Center or aboutads.info.
  • If you use a browser with tracking protection or Global Privacy Control (GPC) enabled, third-party ad cookies are restricted automatically according to your browser settings.

4. Hosting Infrastructure

Our static pages are hosted via GitHub Pages. Like nearly all web hosting providers, GitHub may process standard technical connection metadata (such as your IP address and requested page URL) temporarily to deliver files securely over HTTPS and protect against abuse.

5. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our Contact page or email editorial@browserclues.live.
